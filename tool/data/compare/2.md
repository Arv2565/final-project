| Aspect | Kerala | Karnataka |
|------|------|------|
| Basic policy | Actively runs state lotteries as a major, regulated source of revenue. | Has chosen to **ban lotteries** and declare the state a “lottery-free zone.” |
| Main legal framework | Lotteries (Regulation) Act, 1998 (Central Act), implemented through Kerala State Lotteries and On-line Lotteries (Regulation) Rules, 2003 and Kerala Paper Lotteries (Regulation) Rules, 2005. | Lotteries (Regulation) Act, 1998 (Central), but used mainly to justify state notifications prohibiting all forms of lottery and making Karnataka a lottery-free zone. |
| Scope of state rules | Rules apply to the **whole state of Kerala**, regulate how Kerala’s own lotteries are run (agents, draws, commissions, printing, prize claims, etc.). | No state lottery scheme; state uses its power to prohibit lotteries rather than regulate them; central Act still applies but only to support prohibition. |
| Status of Kerala’s own lotteries in that state | Kerala’s own paper and (earlier) online lotteries are fully legal and regulated; there is a State Lotteries Department and dedicated audit manual for revenue from lotteries. | Karnataka does **not** run its own lottery; past experiments with state lotteries were discontinued, and the state moved to a complete ban. |
| Sale of other states’ lotteries | Kerala has at times restricted or regulated marketing of other states’ online lotteries within Kerala (e.g., litigation over Kerala’s 2003 rules and notices against Sikkim/Karnataka online lotteries). | High Court upheld state notification banning all forms of lotteries and declaring Karnataka a lottery-free zone, thereby stopping sale of other states’ tickets inside Karnataka. |
| Current all-India classification | Listed among the small group (about 10–13) of states where sale of state lottery tickets is **legal and regulated**. [indiatoday] | Cited as a state that **previously had lotteries but now bans sale of lottery tickets**, i.e., one of the lottery-prohibited states. |
| Practical situation for residents | Residents can lawfully buy Kerala government lottery tickets; draws and results are widely publicised and form a routine part of state revenue and welfare funding. | Residents cannot lawfully buy government lottery tickets within Karnataka; lottery business is treated as banned gambling under state policy and HC-upheld notifications. |
